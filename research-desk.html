Methodology

Growth inputs use decimals: 0.05 is 5% annually; 0.05 + 0.01*t is 5%, 6%, 7%… EPS growth starts at t=0 in year one. Dividend growth starts at t=0 for the increase from year one to year two.

P/E moves in equal steps from purchase price / starting EPS to exit P/E. Annual year-end dividends buy fractional shares at that year's modeled price. Cash-held dividends earn 0%. Taxes and fees are excluded.

Discounted value includes separately received dividends and the terminal share price. IRR solves the return on those cash flows; reinvested CAGR also depends on the modeled reinvestment prices. Positive earnings only; EPS growth includes any share-count changes.
