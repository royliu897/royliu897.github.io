# Unmapped-tag sweep

Checked October 29, 2026. This is a sample-based mapping inventory, not a completeness or accounting audit.

## Coverage

Twelve SEC-directory tickers were attempted. Ten had an annual anchor suitable for this pass. Across those ten, the sweep found **1,046 distinct qualified tags outside the current report registry**. That is not 1,046 missing statement rows: most candidates are footnotes, tax reconciliations, lease maturity schedules, overlapping totals, or supplemental disclosures.

The source-native descriptions are retained in the machine-readable inventory generated by the sweep script. Standard tag definitions can be checked against [FASB's taxonomy](https://fasb.org/page/detail?pageId=%2Fprojects%2FFASB-Taxonomies%2F2025-gaap-financial-reporting-taxonomy.html). Definitions alone do not establish that a particular observation belongs to a primary statement.

## Recurring tags that should not become aliases

- Cash including restricted cash occurs in nine issuers. It is not interchangeable with unrestricted cash used in valuation.
- Cash income taxes paid occurs in nine. It is not accrual income tax expense.
- Gross deferred-tax liabilities occurs in nine. It is not necessarily a net deferred-tax balance or a current liability.
- Operating lease liabilities occur frequently. They are not finance-lease liabilities merely because both contain “lease.”
- Authorized or issued shares are not current shares outstanding, and none are weighted-average EPS shares.
- Tax-rate reconciliations and lease maturity schedules recur widely, but do not belong in the existing primary-statement template merely because their tags are common.

## How to use the results

Prioritize candidates by source-statement role, whether the corresponding canonical row is blank, and recurrence across issuers. Keep raw unmapped observations visible, with their units, dates, definition and filing accession. Label them “not directly mapped,” rather than claiming their amounts were completely unused: some contribute indirectly to residual calculations.

An AI mapping assistant could propose a target plus reasoning, source evidence, scope constraints, sign and aggregation rules. Its proposals should remain separate from active mappings until those constraints pass. A high tag-match count is not evidence of financial correctness.

Reproduce from the website repository:

```sh
node scripts/sweep-unmapped-tags.mjs /tmp/unmapped-tag-sweep.json
```

The script spaces source requests and retains small source fixtures in `tests/fixtures/unmapped-sweep-*.json`. It does not edit the registry or activate mappings. The source feed excludes many custom/dimensional facts and untagged tables, so this inventory cannot measure complete filing coverage.
