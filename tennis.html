Outside the lab / Tennis

Years on court

Practice, competition, and being part of a team.

The routine

For years, tennis meant three-hour practices after school alongside coursework. That schedule made preparation a daily habit, whether or not a tournament was coming up.

During a match

I can’t choose what comes across the net. I can choose how I prepare for the next point. Tennis has taught me to return to that smaller task when a match isn’t going my way.

Playing for a team

I played first singles for West Windsor–Plainsboro High School South and helped the team win the county championship and reach the state finals.

  • Third place, USTA Zonal National Competition
  • First singles, WW-P High School South
  • County championship
  • State finals appearance
