Tennis

Tennis was a big part of high school for me: daily three-hour practices after school, weekly varsity team matches, and weekend USTA tournaments.

High school team

My freshman year, I played first doubles for West Windsor–Plainsboro High School, it was a great introduction to tennis as a team sport, and my doubles partner and I won the Mercer County doubles championship. The next year, I played first singles, and helped the team win the Mercer County team championship and reach the state finals.

  • Third place, USTA Zonal National Competition
  • First singles, WW-P High School South
  • County championship
  • State finals appearance
